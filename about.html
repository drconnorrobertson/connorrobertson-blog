About Connor Robertson

I build businesses with AI. Not in theory. In practice, every single day.

I run multiple companies. I host The Prospecting Show, a podcast focused on business development and growth strategy. I founded Elixir Consulting to help business owners implement systems that actually scale.

And I use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, custom agents, MCP servers, automation workflows) to do in hours what used to take weeks.

Why This Blog Exists

Most AI content online falls into two categories: hype pieces that tell you AI will change everything (without showing you how), and deeply technical tutorials written for machine learning engineers.

There is almost nothing for business owners who want to know: how do I actually use this? What buttons do I press? What does the workflow look like? What will it cost me? How long will it take?

That is what this blog covers. Every post is written from real experience running real businesses. When I write about automating email with Claude, it is because I actually did it and I am going to show you exactly how. When I compare GPT-4 to Claude for coding, it is because I have used both extensively and tracked the results.

What You Will Find Here

Step-by-step tutorials on Claude, ChatGPT, and AI agents. Actual code snippets you can copy and use. Honest comparisons between tools. Workflows I use daily to run businesses more efficiently. SEO strategies powered by AI. And practical guides on building AI agents that do useful work.

No fluff. No affiliate-driven recommendations. No theoretical hand-waving. Just the playbook I use, shared openly.
